Privacy Policy

Scars of the Last Kingdom — Last updated: May 2026

No data collected

Scars of the Last Kingdom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The game stores save data locally on your device only. Nothing leaves your device.

1. Who We Are

Scars of the Last Kingdom is an independent game. This privacy policy describes how the game handles data on your device.

2. What Data Is Stored

The game stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Game save data — your progress through the story, choices made, endings discovered, and current survival stats
  • Settings — display and audio preferences you have set inside the game
  • Content warning flag — a single flag indicating whether you have acknowledged the content warning screen

All of this data is stored using your device’s local storage system (AsyncStorage on Android). It never leaves your device.

3. What Data Is Not Collected

  • No account is created or required
  • No personal information is collected (no name, email, phone, or address)
  • No location data is collected
  • No device identifiers are collected
  • No analytics or tracking are present in the current build
  • No advertising SDKs are present
  • No crash reporting is present in the current build
  • No data is transmitted to any server

4. Internet Permissions

The game does not require an internet connection to play. All story content, images, and game logic are bundled with the app at install time. The app does not make any network requests during normal gameplay.

5. Children

Scars of the Last Kingdom is not intended for children under 16. The game contains dark fantasy themes including violence, death, and mental health subject matter. See the content warning inside the app for full details.

Because no personal data is collected from anyone, the protections of COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar laws are satisfied by default.

6. Third-Party Services

The current version of the game does not use any third-party services, SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms. If this changes in a future update, this policy will be updated before the update is released.

7. Data Security

Save data is stored in the standard Android app storage sandbox. It is protected by Android’s normal app isolation. The app declares allowBackup: false, which prevents your save data from being included in Android’s automatic cloud backup system.

8. Deleting Your Data

You can delete all locally stored data at any time directly inside the app:

  • Open the Settings panel from the main menu
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Reset All Data
  • Confirm the action in the dialog that appears

This permanently wipes all save data, settings, and the content warning flag. Alternatively, uninstalling the app removes all local data automatically.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a material way — for example, if analytics or crash reporting are added in a future release — the updated policy will be published here before the update reaches Google Play. Continued use of the app after a policy change indicates acceptance of the new terms.
