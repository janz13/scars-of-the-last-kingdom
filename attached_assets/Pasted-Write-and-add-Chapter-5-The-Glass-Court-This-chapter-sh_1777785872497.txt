Chapter 5 title:
The Glass Court

Main theme:
Luxury above starvation. Power, beauty, lies, class divide, medical experiments, political survival, and moral corruption.

Chapter feel:
The Glass Court should feel completely different from the tunnels and Iron Saints territory.

The tunnels were dark and desperate.
The Iron Saints were brutal and religious.
The Glass Court should be elegant, cold, bright, beautiful, and cruel.

The people above the ruined city live behind sealed glass while everyone below starves.

Important NPCs:

1. Lady Seraphine Vale
Elegant ruler of the Glass Court. She treats survival like theater and enjoys watching desperate people make impossible choices.

2. Director Enoch Varr
A scientist studying relic marks, corruption, memory loss, and the player’s wrist mark.

3. Juno
A servant who secretly helps people from below. Juno can become an ally, informant, or casualty depending on player choices.

4. Lord Cael Orison
A charming noble who may manipulate or aid the player. He is useful, dangerous, and never fully honest.

5. The Pale Curator
A collector of relic-touched humans. The Curator recognizes the player and may know something about their past.

6. The Masked Heir
A mysterious figure connected to the player’s lost memory and the truth behind the blackout.
