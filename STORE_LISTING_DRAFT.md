## Full Description

**Scars of the Last Kingdom** is a dark dystopian fantasy survival visual novel RPG about impossible choices, fractured alliances, and the cost of staying alive in a world that has already ended.

The blackout came without warning. The surface is a wasteland of ash and ruin. Below, the metro tunnels hold the last remnants of civilization — factions fighting over scraps, companions whose loyalty depends on how far you're willing to go, and a mark on your wrist that pulses with something you don't understand.

Every choice matters. Manage survival stats — health, hunger, thirst, morale, corruption — as they erode or build across the story. Decide who to trust: Mira, the pragmatic survivor, or Cain, the calculating opportunist. Navigate five rival factions, from the militaristic Iron Saints to the secretive Last Archive. Roll dice on desperate gambles where a critical failure can end your run.

**What's in the game right now:**

- **1,400+ story nodes** across multiple chapters of branching narrative
- **200+ endings** — death, betrayal, sacrifice, faction, corruption, secret, and the rare true ending
- **Survival stats** that shape how scenes unfold and which paths open
- **Two companions** (Mira and Cain) with trust systems and story arcs
- **Five factions** with reputation that unlocks and locks story branches
- **Dice checks** on risky choices with critical success/failure outcomes
- **Hallucination events** triggered by corruption that warp the narrative
- **Route Codex** tracking your journey history and discovered endings
- **Pixel-art backgrounds** for every scene — ruined stations, dark corridors, surface wasteland
- **Auto-save** with manual reset options
- **Offline play** — all story data is bundled, no internet needed mid-game
- **Content warning** screen on first launch

**What this game is NOT:**
This is a text-driven visual novel RPG. It does not have open-world exploration, real-time combat, multiplayer, or 3D graphics. The focus is on story, consequence, and replayability.

Carry the mark. Make the choice. Survive what remains.

## Content Warning Text (shown in-app on first launch)

⚠ **Content Warning**

This game contains dark fantasy themes that may be distressing:

- Violence and death — combat, execution, and survival deaths
- Mental health — morale collapse, despair, and psychological pressure
- Moral ambiguity — choices with no clean answer
- Loss — companions can die or betray you based on your decisions
- Corruption — body horror themes as the mark changes you
- Disturbing imagery — described through text, not shown visually

Player discretion is advised. You can reset your progress at any time from Settings.
