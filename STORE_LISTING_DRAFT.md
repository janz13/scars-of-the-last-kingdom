## Short Description
A dark dystopian fantasy survival RPG. Make choices, gather companions, and fight for what remains.

## Full Description
**Scars of the Last Kingdom** is a dark dystopian fantasy survival visual novel RPG about impossible choices, fractured alliances, and the cost of staying alive.

Set in a ruined world of collapsing factions, haunted shelters, and dangerous expeditions, the game puts you in control of a story shaped by branching decisions and survival pressure. Every run can lead to different outcomes, hidden routes, and multiple endings.

Build through tense story scenes, manage survival stats, and decide who to trust as you navigate rival factions and encounter potential companions. Some paths reward caution, others reward risk, and some are only discovered by making difficult choices.

### Features
- Branching narrative choices with meaningful consequences
- Multiple endings and route discovery
- Survival stats that shape how scenes unfold
- Companions and faction-driven story paths
- Dark fantasy setting with dystopian atmosphere
- Pixel-art backgrounds and visual novel presentation
- Content warning screen for mature themes
- Offline-friendly local save data

This is a story-driven game focused on atmosphere, consequence, and replayability. It does not promise open-world exploration, real-time combat, or multiplayer features.
